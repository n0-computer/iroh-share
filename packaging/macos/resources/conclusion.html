Iroh Share is installed

Open Iroh Share from the Applications folder in your home directory.

The daemon starts at login and keeps running after the app closes. Background permissions can be managed in System Settings.

To open blake3.net and pkarr.net links through the built-in gateway, install the iroh link browser extension for Chrome or Brave from the Chrome Web Store. The Firefox version is awaiting review; until then you can load it as a temporary add-on.

To uninstall, close the app and open Uninstall Iroh Share.command in the same folder. Your saved state and shared/downloaded files will be kept.
