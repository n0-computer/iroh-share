Iroh Share for your account

Installs Iroh Share in your home folder's Applications directory and starts a background daemon at login. No administrator access is required.

A new desktop configuration is connected automatically. Existing connections and saved data are preserved.

Closing the desktop app leaves the daemon running. macOS may ask you to allow background activity or access to folders you share.

To open blake3.net and pkarr.net links through the built-in gateway, install the iroh link browser extension for Chrome or Brave from the Chrome Web Store. The Firefox version is awaiting review; until then you can load it as a temporary add-on.
